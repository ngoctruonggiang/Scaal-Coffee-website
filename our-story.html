our story

Scaal has been brewing boldness and conversation since 1955. We treat every roast as a celebration of community.

The Scaal story begins with a simple desire—to share a remarkable cup of coffee with friends. Over the decades, the company grew from a single roastery into a collective of farmers, roasters, and artists who bring their craft to every bean we sell.

Today, Scaal Coffee sources beans from sustainable farms across the globe. Our small-batch roasting technique coaxes out the subtle flavors unique to each region. We believe that savoring a cup should be a ritual, not a rush.

Visit one of our tasting rooms or sign up for a roastery tour to experience how we transform green beans into the blends you love. We’re proud to be part of your daily routine.
